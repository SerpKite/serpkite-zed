SerpKite gives the Agent Panel Google search (web, news, images, videos, maps, places, shopping, scholar, patents, autocomplete) and public webpages, extraction and site crawls as LLM-ready Markdown, plus cited answers and deep research reports.

You can connect in two ways:

- **OAuth (default):** leave `serpkite_api_key` empty. On first use a browser window opens to sign in to SerpKite and approve Zed.
- **API key:** create a key at [app.serpkite.com/keys](https://app.serpkite.com/keys) and set `serpkite_api_key` to it.

New accounts get 1,000 free credits, plus 1,000 every month. Failed and empty searches are not billed. Docs: [serpkite.com/docs/mcp](https://serpkite.com/docs/mcp)
